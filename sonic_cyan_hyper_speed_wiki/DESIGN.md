## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for an energetic, mobile-first fandom archive dedicated to high-speed lore, gaming records, and character databases. Built to transform complex, deep wiki architectures into an exhilarating, streamlined mobile experience, it fuses crisp game-inspired aesthetics with structured utility.

### Personality & Tone
- **Hyper-Kinetic yet Disciplined:** Fast-paced, punchy, and modern without devolving into visual chaos. The interface prioritizes scannable typography, dense data tables, and swift navigational switching.
- **Electric Nostalgia meets Contemporary UI:** Evokes the thrill of acceleration, classic blue-blur momentum, and golden rings through vibrant modern hues paired with deep, immersive contrast.
- **Authoritative & Enthusiast-Driven:** Delivers the depth of an encyclopedic compendium with the polish of a premier mobile gaming client.

### Design Movement
**Modern Kinetic Futurism with Glass & Tonal Layering.** The aesthetic relies on deep navy structural surfaces, radiant cyan focal points, gleaming ring-gold accents, crisp micro-borders, and tactile data chips.

## Colors

The palette leverages a deep navy foundational canvas that allows brilliant cyan and energetic ring-gold to surge forward with immediate visual hierarchy.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#69CFFF` - Sonic Cyan):** Key call-to-actions, active tab indicators, hyperlinked wiki references, interactive progress fills, and primary glow highlights.
- **Secondary (`#FFD200` - Ring Gold):** Special achievements, canonical highlights, stat bar peaks, VIP/featured badges, and star ratings.
- **Tertiary (`#102A43` - Deep Royal Navy Surface):** Elevated cards, stat panels, list item backgrounds, segmented controls, and drawer sheets.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#0A192F` - Abyss Void):** Default viewport background, ensuring high contrast and minimal battery drain on OLED mobile displays.
- **Surface Variant & Borders (`#1E3A5F` / `#2D4F7C`):** Subdued boundary rules, inactive progress bar tracks, and divider strokes.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-priority titles, key metrics, and primary glyphs.
- **Muted Steel (`#8FA6C1`):** Secondary metadata, timestamps, author notes, and inactive tab labels.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs **Outfit**—a streamlined, geometric sans with kinetic, aerodynamic curvature—for display, headlines, and data badges with **Inter** for dense, comfortable encyclopedic readability.

- **Display & Headings (Outfit):** Set in bold and extra-bold weights. Character titles, franchise eras, and transformation names utilize slight negative letter-spacing to reinforce speed and momentum.
- **Body & Lore Content (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral, and systematically balanced. Generous line heights are maintained to sustain comfort across lengthy game synopses and history logs.
- **Labels, Badges, and Stats (Outfit):** Uppercase tracking applied to micro-labels, stat attributes (e.g., `SPEED`, `ACCEL`, `FIRST APPEARANCE`), and filtering tags.

## Layout & Spacing

A compact, fluid layout system engineered specifically for swift single-handed mobile navigation and information density.

### Grid & Responsive Mechanics
- **Mobile Handheld (320px – 480px):** Single-column content stack with an outer screen margin of `margin` (`1rem`). Category cards and character rosters utilize a 2-column responsive auto-grid with `gutter-sm` (`0.75rem`).
- **Phablet & Foldable (481px – 768px):** Expands to a multi-column card system with `margin-lg` (`1.5rem`) margin and persistent side navigation rails.
- **Sticky App Structure:** Fixed top app bar with omni-search input and quick-jump filters; fixed bottom navigation bar optimized for thumb-reach ergonomics.
- **Vertical Cadence:** Strict 4px base rhythm. Text blocks use `space-md` gaps; card internal padding defaults to `space-lg` (`1rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows in favor of luminous atmospheric glows, high-contrast dark tonal layering, and fine boundary strokes.

### Tonal Tiers
- **Base Canvas (Level 0):** `#0A192F` — Background plane.
- **Tonal Container (Level 1):** `#102A43` — Standard wiki content cards, collapsible headers, and table rows with a subtle border stroke (`1px solid rgba(105, 207, 255, 0.12)`).
- **Floating Modals & Sheets (Level 2):** `#143555` — Bottom drawers, quick-search overlays, and floating action rings with a diffuse rim shadow (`0 8px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`).
- **Interactive Focus & Cyan Halo:** Interactive touch targets gain an active ambient cyan flare: `0 0 16px rgba(105, 207, 255, 0.35)`.
- **Gold Ring Focus:** Critical quest markers, emerald counters, and canonical highlights receive an amber-gold ambient radiance: `0 0 14px rgba(255, 210, 0, 0.3)`.

## Shapes

The geometry balances sleek aerodynamic velocity with clean digital structure. 

- **Primary Geometry:** `roundedness: 2` delivers a standard corner radius of `0.5rem` (8px), scaling to `1rem` (16px) for cards (`rounded-lg`) and `1.5rem` (24px) for full-width modals and dialog sheets (`rounded-xl`).
- **Speed Fillets & Pills:** Chips, status tags, pill tabs, and stat bar fills use full pill geometry (`9999px`) to evoke physical velocity tracks, capsule pods, and rings.
- **Subtle Slanted Cuts:** Accent ribbons, category tags, and stat labels can leverage a 6-degree forward italic slant (`transform: skewX(-6deg)`) to convey physical forward propulsion.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Sonic Dash"):** Solid `#69CFFF` fill with deep navy text (`#0A192F`), bold `Outfit` uppercase lettering, full pill radius, and a rapid scale-down touch feedback (`transform: scale(0.97)`).
- **Secondary ("Ring Action"):** Deep `#102A43` fill with a `1.5px` border in `#FFD200`, `#FFD200` text, and subtle golden ring glow on active states.
- **Ghost / Utility:** Transparent fill with `#8FA6C1` icons/text, brightening to `#69CFFF` on tap.

### Badges & Category Chips
- Compact pill-shaped metadata tags with dark translucent backing (`rgba(16, 42, 67, 0.8)`), a `1px` border matching the thematic category (e.g., `#69CFFF` for Game Canon, `#FFD200` for Super Forms, `#FF5964` for Antagonists), and bold `label-sm` Outfit typography.

### Stat Bars (Velocity & Attribute Meters)
- Segmented or smooth horizontal progress tracks with a dark navy trough (`#1E3A5F`).
- Dynamic value fills utilizing gradient steps from `#69CFFF` to `#FFD200`, rounded terminal caps, and an accompanying numeric read-out set in `label-md`.

### Quick Search & Omni-Bar
- Prominent mobile search input docked to the app bar. Dark container (`#102A43`) with `0.5rem` corners, magnifying icon in `#69CFFF`, placeholder text in `#8FA6C1`, and an instant clear button (`X`).

### Navigation & Tab Bars
- **Bottom Navigation:** Fixed bottom bar with blurred backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`), `#0A192F` 90% opacity, featuring icon + micro-label layouts with an animated cyan neon indicator dot under active items.
- **Segmented Wiki Tabs (e.g., Overview, Gameplay, History, Gallery):** Horizontal scrolling pill tab bar with smooth snap sliding and high-contrast active states.

### Category & Character Cards
- Tonal card backgrounds (`#102A43`) featuring an edge-to-edge media crop with bottom-gradient fade, prominent Outfit headline, era badge, and clean chevron indicator. Outlined with `1px solid rgba(105, 207, 255, 0.15)`.

### Infobox Tables
- Structured, alternating key-value lists displaying game appearances, voice actors, abilities, and affiliations with border separators in `#1E3A5F`. Keys formatted in `label-sm` (`#8FA6C1`); values formatted in `body-sm` (`#FFFFFF`).
